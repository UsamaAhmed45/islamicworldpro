Each page contains exactly the ayahs of the same page in the 604-page Madinah Mushaf, so page numbers, juz and surah starts line up with the printed copy. Line breaks follow your screen width.
Use the arrow buttons, swipe on a phone or tablet, or press the left and right arrow keys. As in a printed Mushaf, the left arrow moves forward.
