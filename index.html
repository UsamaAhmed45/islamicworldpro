About the app

Everything a Muslim needs, in one calm app

From Fajr to Isha, Islamic World Pro keeps the Qur'an, your Azkar, prayer guidance and the Hadith close at hand — with a Tajweed colour-coded Mushaf, an Imam Bot that answers your questions, and voice search everywhere you need it. Clear typography, offline-friendly reading, and no clutter.
