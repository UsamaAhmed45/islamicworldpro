Accept from us

Do not burden us beyond our strength

A Comprehensive Dua of the Prophet ﷺ

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى، وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

One of the Prophet's ﷺ most comprehensive personal supplications, combining worldly and religious good.
