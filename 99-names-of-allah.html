99 Names — questions

Asma-ul-Husna — “the most beautiful names” — are the names of Allah mentioned in the Qur'an and Sunnah, such as Ar-Rahman (the Most Gracious), Al-Malik (the King) and Al-Quddus (the Most Holy). The Prophet ﷺ said Allah has ninety-nine names and whoever learns them will enter Paradise (Sahih al-Bukhari 7392, Sahih Muslim 2677).
The list and order follow the narration recorded by at-Tirmidhi (3507), the list most widely memorised. Scholars note that Allah's names are not limited to ninety-nine.
The Prophet ﷺ said: “I am Muhammad, I am Ahmad, I am al-Mahi through whom Allah erases disbelief, I am al-Hashir and I am al-'Aqib” (Sahih al-Bukhari 3532). The Asma-un-Nabi tab lists these with titles given to him in the Qur'an, each with its source.
