Qur'an, Translation & Tafseer

Read, listen and understand the whole Qur'an — four reading modes, three tafseers and audio for every ayah, all stored on your phone.

Complete Qur'an — 114 Surahs, 30 Paras

The full Mushaf in Arabic. Browse chapter by chapter (Surah) or part by part (Juz / Para) — whichever way you already read.

Uthmani Text mode

The clean, classical Uthmani script on its own, with no colours or extra layers — the way a printed Mushaf looks.

Tajweedi Qur'an — colour-coded recitation

Every Tajweed rule shown in colour directly on the Arabic, so you can see exactly how each letter should be pronounced as you read.

Tarjuma & Tafseer mode

Arabic verse followed by its meaning in plain Urdu and English, with verse-by-verse tafseer explaining the context and lesson.

Word-by-Word Qur'an

Tap through the Arabic word by word and see what each individual word means — the fastest way to start understanding Qur'anic Arabic.

Three complete tafseers

Tafsir Ibn Kathir, Ma'ariful Qur'an and Bayan-ul-Quran, available inline beneath every ayah.

Urdu & English translation

Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry's classic Urdu translation alongside a clear English rendering — switch or show both.

Audio recitation for every ayah

Play a single verse or let the recitation run continuously through the Surah, with the current ayah highlighted as it is recited.

Verse & topic search

Jump to any ayah by reference (for example 2:255) or search by topic — patience, forgiveness, parents — and go straight to the verses.

Bookmarks & personal notes

Save any ayah while you read and attach your own reflection to it, so you can return to what mattered to you.

100% offline Qur'an

The Arabic text, translations, tafseer and audio are bundled inside the app — no internet, no data charges, no waiting.

Imam Bot, Voice & Search

Ask a question the way you would ask a person — by typing or by speaking, in English or Urdu — and get the answer from inside the app.

Imam Bot — AI Islamic assistant

A built-in assistant with a curated knowledge base of over 215 questions and answers covering worship, belief, daily practice and app help.

Answers sourced from the app itself

Imam Bot answers from the Qur'an, Hadith and dua content already inside Islamic World Pro — not from the open internet. For personal rulings it points you to a qualified local scholar.

Bilingual replies — English & Urdu

Every answer exists in both languages and follows whichever language you have the app set to.

Ask by voice

Tap the microphone and simply speak your question instead of typing it.

Global voice search

Search the whole app by voice — an ayah, a Surah name, a hadith number, a dua or a tool — and jump straight there.

Search across everything

One search box that looks through the Qur'an, all nine Hadith collections, Azkar, duas and every screen in the app at once.

Hadith by number

Type or say a hadith number and open that exact narration instantly.

Floating Ask Imam button

The assistant is one tap away from any screen, in a full-screen overlay that keeps your place in the app.

Qur'anic root search

Search the Qur'an the way Arabic actually works — by trilateral root. Enter ك ت ب, or any word built on it such as كتاب, and every Qur'anic word from that root is found across all 6,236 ayahs.

Root search in English letters

Do not have an Arabic keyboard? Type the root in Latin letters — ktb, rahm, slm — and the app maps it to the correct Arabic root for you.

Speak an Arabic root

Voice search accepts spoken Arabic roots and verse words too, whichever language the interface is set to.

Prayer Times, Qibla & Tracking

Everything around Salah in one place — accurate timings, the direction to face, and a gentle record of what you have prayed.

Accurate Salah times

Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha plus sunrise, calculated for your exact coordinates using standard calculation methods.

Location-aware, anywhere in the world

Times follow your GPS position automatically, with a manual location option when you prefer to set it yourself.

Next-prayer countdown

The home screen always shows which prayer is next and exactly how long is left, with a live progress ring.

Azaan alerts & prayer notifications

A notification at every prayer time with the adhan sound, delivered reliably even when the app is closed.

Full-screen prayer alerts

On newer Android versions the alert can take over the screen so you never miss a prayer while your phone is locked.

Live Qibla compass

A real-time compass that uses your device sensors and true bearing calculation to point you towards the Ka'bah, correct in any orientation.

Prayer Tracker with streaks

Mark each Salah as you pray it and watch your streak grow, with weekly and monthly progress charts at a glance.

Qada (missed prayer) tracker

Log missed obligatory prayers by type and tick them off as you make them up — with optional check-in reminders and no pressure.

Step-by-step Salah guide

The correct method for every prayer with rak'ah counts, positions and what to recite — ideal for new Muslims and for children.

12-hour or 24-hour time

Show prayer timings in whichever clock format you are used to.

Hadith Library

Nine complete collections — around 42,000 narrations — browsable, searchable and available without an internet connection.

Sahih al-Bukhari — 7,589 hadiths

The full collection, organised by its classical 97-book division, with chapter headings in English and Urdu.

Sahih Muslim — 7,563 hadiths

Complete and browsable book by book, with reference numbers matching standard published editions.

Sunan Abu Dawud — 5,274 hadiths

The complete Sunan, chaptered and searchable.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi — 3,998 hadiths

Including the gradings Imam at-Tirmidhi records for each narration.

Sunan an-Nasa'i — 5,765 hadiths

The full collection with its chapter structure preserved.

Sunan Ibn Majah — 4,343 hadiths

The sixth of the Kutub as-Sittah, complete.

Muwatta Imam Malik — 1,858 hadiths

The earliest of the great compilations, complete with its chapters.

Musnad Ahmad — 1,374 hadiths

A selection from the Musnad of Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal.

Mishkat al-Masabih — 4,428 hadiths

The classic topical arrangement drawn from the major collections.

English & Urdu for every narration

Read each hadith in Arabic with a translation in either language, or show both together.

Grading, narrator & reference

Every narration carries its authenticity grading, the companion who narrated it, its book, chapter and reference number.

Favourites & keyword search

Star any hadith to find it again, and search the entire library by keyword, topic or number.

Azkar, Duas & Dhikr

Authentic remembrance for every part of the day, with a Tasbih counter built for real use.

Morning & evening Azkar

The complete morning and evening remembrances with Arabic, transliteration and meaning, in the order they are traditionally recited.

Full dua library by occasion

Authentic duas grouped by situation — daily life, travel, weather, health, protection, emotions, family, wealth, knowledge, Ramadan, Hajj and death.

Qur'anic & Prophetic duas

The supplications of Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Yunus, Ayyub, Zakariyya, Sulayman and 'Isa (peace be upon them), and the comprehensive duas of the Prophet ﷺ.

Arabic, transliteration, English & Urdu

Every dua shown four ways so you can read it, pronounce it and understand it — whichever you need.

Authenticity & source for each dua

The grading, collection, hadith number, chapter and narrator are shown with every supplication.

Digital Tasbih counter

A large tap counter with a live progress arc, preset dhikr modes, adjustable targets and automatic reset for each set.

Haptics & tap sound

Vibration feedback and a soft click on every count, so you can keep your dhikr going without looking at the screen.

Tasbih history & daily goal

A seven-day chart of your dhikr and a daily goal to work towards.

Asma-ul-Husna — 99 Names of Allah

All ninety-nine names with Arabic, transliteration and meaning, presented for daily reflection and memorisation.

Guides & Learning

Clear, step-by-step guidance for the moments that matter — and a proper course for learning to recite.

Hajj & Umrah guide

A complete walkthrough of both pilgrimages — Ihram, Talbiyah, Tawaf, Sa'i, Arafah, Muzdalifah, Mina and the farewell Tawaf — with the correct dua at each stage.

Namaz-e-Janaza guide

The funeral prayer explained step by step, with every takbir and the duas to recite — written to be followed calmly in the moment.

Norani Qaida — full 17-chapter course

The complete classic Qaida, page by page as originally printed, with recitation audio for every line.

Tajweed rules

Noon Sakinah and Tanween, Meem Sakinah, Qalqalah, Madd and the rest — each rule explained with examples and audio.

Makharij pronunciation guide

Where every Arabic letter is produced in the mouth and throat, with sound for each one.

Offline learning audio

All Qaida, Tajweed and pronunciation audio is bundled in the app — it works with no internet and costs nothing to play.

Kids Corner

A separate, gentle space for children: their first prayers, short Surahs, easy duas, Prophetic stories, quizzes and a memory game.

Calendar, Ramadan, Zakat & Sadaqah

Keep the Islamic year, the month of fasting and your giving all in one place.

Hijri calendar

A full Islamic calendar shown side by side with Gregorian dates, month by month.

Islamic occasions

Ramadan, the two Eids, Ashura, Laylat al-Qadr, the Islamic New Year and other significant days marked on the calendar.

Today's Hijri date in the header

The current Hijri date and weekday sit at the top of the home screen, so you always know where you are in the Islamic month.

Ramadan Tracker

A day-by-day fasting calendar for the whole month, with each fast logged as kept or missed.

Sehri & Iftar countdown

A live countdown to the next Sehri and Iftar time throughout Ramadan.

Zakat calculator

Work out your annual Zakat on cash, savings, gold, silver and business assets in a few taps, with the current Nisab built in.

Sadaqah log

Record every act of charity with amount, currency, date and an optional note, and keep a running history of your giving.

Nearby: Mosques & Halal Food

Find what you need around you, wherever you happen to be.

Nearby mosque finder

Masjids close to your current location, with distance and directions — useful at home and essential when travelling.

Halal restaurant finder

Halal places to eat near you, so you can find a meal in an unfamiliar city without guessing.

Halal grocery & butcher finder

Halal grocery shops and butchers nearby, filtered by the type of place you need.

Daily Life, Reminders & Personal Records

Small, steady nudges through the day, and a private record of your own worship.

Daily Posts feed

A refreshed feed of duas, hadith and short reminders to keep the day grounded.

Daily reminder notifications

Optional daily nudges for Qur'an reading, a hadith, an ayah and your Azkar — turn them all on with one tap.

Friday Al-Kahf reminder

A weekly reminder to read Surah al-Kahf on Jumu'ah.

Qada check-in reminders

A gentle periodic prompt to log or make up missed prayers, if you choose to enable it.

Bookmarks & notes

Every saved ayah and every note you have written, gathered in one place.

Everything stays on your device

Your tracker history, notes, bookmarks and logs are stored locally on your phone.

The App Itself

Built to be calm, fast and usable in places where the connection is not.

Works fully offline

Around 185 MB of Qur'an text, translations, tafseer, Hadith collections and audio ship inside the app. Once installed, almost nothing needs the internet.

English & Urdu throughout

The entire interface — every screen, button, guide and answer — exists in both languages, with proper right-to-left layout for Urdu.

Dark and light themes

A deep emerald and gold night theme, and a bright ivory and gold day theme. One tap switches between them.

Quick Actions home screen

The tools you use most — Qur'an, Azkar, Prayer, Qibla, Tasbih, Hadith, Calendar, Zakat, Kids and more — one tap from the home screen.

Live weather & location

Your city and current temperature sit in the header alongside the date, in °C or °F.

No ads, anywhere

There are no banners, no interstitials and no sponsored content anywhere in the app. Nothing interrupts your reading, your dhikr or your prayer.

No subscription, no in-app purchases

Every feature is included from the moment you install. There is no premium tier, no paywall and nothing locked behind a payment.

No account, no login required

Open the app and start using it. There is no sign-up, no email, no phone number and no password to create.

Free forever on Android

The whole app is free to install and use, published by Aurevia Solution on Google Play as com.aurevia.islamicworld.
